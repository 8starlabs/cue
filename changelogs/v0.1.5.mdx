## Fixed

- Tab now falls back to the active zsh completion widget when Cue has no suggestion.
- Escape dismisses Cue while preserving vi insert mode’s normal switch to command mode.
- Cue keeps a custom `KEYTIMEOUT` instead of overwriting it during startup.

Thanks to [Ducksss](https://github.com/Ducksss) for reporting the Tab fallback and vi-mode keyboard issues in [#2](https://github.com/8starlabs/cue/issues/2) and [#3](https://github.com/8starlabs/cue/issues/3).
