## Added

- Add `cue-update` to update Homebrew and Cue, stop stale app and worker processes for the current user, clear the stale socket, and reload the updated integration in the current shell.

## Fixed

- Keep Homebrew's stable `opt/cue` path in zsh so open terminals can refresh Cue after an upgrade without retaining a deleted Cellar path.
